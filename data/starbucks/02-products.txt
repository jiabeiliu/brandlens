Source: Starbucks official company profile (starbucks.com, summarized Oct 2026).

Starbucks' product range spans in-store, at-home and on-the-go:

Coffee: more than 30 blends and single-origin premium coffees, sourced with ethical sourcing practices; buyers travel to coffee farms in Latin America, Africa and Asia.

Handcrafted beverages: fresh-brewed coffee, hot and iced espresso beverages, slow-steeped Starbucks Cold Brew, Frappuccino blended beverages, Starbucks Refreshers, smoothies and teas.

Fresh food: baked pastries, sandwiches, salads, protein boxes and bowls, oatmeal, yogurt parfaits and fruit cups.

Merchandise: coffee- and tea-brewing equipment, mugs, accessories, packaged goods and gifts.

Consumer (grocery) products: whole-bean and ground coffee, Starbucks VIA instant coffee, K-Cup pods, and ready-to-drink bottled beverages including bottled Frappuccino, iced coffee, Doubleshot espresso drinks and bottled Cold Brew. Starbucks also distributes through partners such as Nestle and PepsiCo.

Seasonal and limited-time offerings are a major traffic driver — the annual Pumpkin Spice season, for example, has produced record-breaking sales weeks. The company also runs brand and merchandise collaborations that have helped revive brand heat during its turnaround.
