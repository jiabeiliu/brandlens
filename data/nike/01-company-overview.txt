Source: Nike, Inc. company profile (public knowledge, summarized Oct 2026).

Nike, Inc. is an American athletic footwear and apparel corporation headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon. It is the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel and a major manufacturer of sports equipment.

The company was founded on January 25, 1964, as "Blue Ribbon Sports" by University of Oregon track athlete Phil Knight and his coach Bill Bowerman. It started as a distributor for Japanese running shoes, selling out of Knight's car at track meets. In 1971 the company launched its own footwear line under the name Nike, taken from the Greek goddess of victory, with the Swoosh logo designed by Carolyn Davidson.

Key facts: Nike is a public company (NYSE: NKE, Dow Jones component). For fiscal year 2025 it reported revenue of about $46.3 billion and employed roughly 77,800 people worldwide. Its sole remaining subsidiary is Converse, acquired in 2003 for $309 million. Previous subsidiaries that were later divested include Cole Haan, Umbro, Hurley, and Bauer Hockey.

Nike's regional sales mix (2023) was roughly: North America 42%, Europe/Middle East/Africa 26%, Greater China 14%, Asia Pacific & Latin America 13%. By product, footwear accounts for about 65% of sales and apparel about 27%.

Leadership: Elliott Hill is president and CEO, leading a turnaround plan that in 2026 included workforce reductions of roughly 1,400 roles (about 2%), concentrated in the technology department.
