Caitlin 1 signature launch (October 2026): Nike officially unveiled the Caitlin 1, the first signature sneaker and apparel collection for WNBA star Caitlin Clark of the Indiana Fever. The shoe debuts a new Nike Opticast upper with cast polyurethane nodes, a forefoot Air Zoom Turbo unit and a Cushlon midsole. Launch colorways "Caitlin Blue" and "Warning Code" were released in China on September 24, 2026, followed by a global launch on October 1, 2026, across roughly 5,000 retail doors — about twice the typical distribution for a Nike basketball signature shoe. CEO Elliott Hill called it the largest women's signature shoe launch in Nike's history. Nike's women's signature basketball business has grown nearly 500% since 2022, with the A'ja Wilson and Sabrina Ionescu lines also performing strongly.

NikeLab Running (October 1, 2026): a new global apparel collection responding to the modern run-club culture boom — technical apparel designed to work for training and for everyday wear. The launch reflects Nike's read that running has become as much a social scene and style statement as a sport.

Swoosh fine jewelry (Holiday 2026): Nike's first official jewelry collection, translating the Swoosh and sneaker silhouettes (Air Force 1, Air Max 1) into sterling silver and gold pieces priced from $165 to $5,750, sold through Nike's official webstore. A move to extend the brand into lifestyle luxury.

NBA Standard Issue Collection (2026-27 season): Nike and the NBA launched a league-wide off-court apparel system across all 30 teams, developed with feedback from 400+ elite high-school players, 100 NBA players and all 30 teams — reconnecting league apparel with basketball culture and fan identity.
