Tesla, Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered at Gigafactory Texas in Austin, Texas. Its mission is accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy — "building a world powered by solar energy, running on batteries and transported by electric vehicles."

The company was incorporated on July 1, 2003, as Tesla Motors by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning, named as a tribute to inventor Nikola Tesla. Elon Musk led the Series A funding round in February 2004, became chairman, and was named CEO in 2008.

Scale: over 100,000 employees worldwide (about 134,800 in 2025 per filings); six huge vertically-integrated factories across three continents (Fremont, Gigafactory Texas, Gigafactory Shanghai, Gigafactory Berlin, plus Nevada and New York operations). 2025 revenue was about $94.8 billion with production output of 1.66 million vehicles and 46.7 GWh of battery energy storage systems.

Tesla designs, builds, sells and services its products in-house with a first-principles engineering culture. Its vehicles are positioned among the safest in the world, and over-the-air software updates regularly add features. In 2023 Tesla reported its products helped avoid about 20.4 million metric tons of CO2e.

Tesla is one of the world's most valuable companies by market capitalization and has been the world's most valuable automaker since July 2020. It trades on NASDAQ under TSLA.
