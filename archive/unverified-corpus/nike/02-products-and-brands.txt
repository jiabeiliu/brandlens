Nike's core product lines include some of the most recognizable sneakers in the world:

- Air Force 1: originally a 1982 basketball shoe, now a lifestyle staple and one of Nike's best-selling silhouettes ever.
- Air Max: launched in 1987 as the first shoe with visible Air cushioning; the Air Max line spans dozens of models.
- Nike Dunk: originally a 1985 basketball shoe, revived as a skateboarding and streetwear icon (Nike SB Dunk).
- Air Jordan: the signature line built around Michael Jordan, operating as the Jordan Brand; a cultural phenomenon spanning basketball performance and streetwear.
- Nike Blazers, Foamposite, Air Huarache: heritage basketball and lifestyle models with loyal followings.
- Nike Running: Pegasus, Vaporfly and Alphafly (carbon-plated marathon racers), plus the NikeLab Running apparel collection launched October 2026, designed to bridge training performance and everyday wear.
- Converse: subsidiary brand known for the Chuck Taylor All Star, serving skate, music and youth culture.

Brand assets: the "Just Do It" slogan (created 1988 by Wieden+Kennedy, Nike's long-time ad agency) and the Swoosh logo are among the most recognized trademarks in sports. In 2020 the Nike brand alone was valued above $32 billion, the most valuable brand among sports businesses.

Nike sells through wholesale partners, its own Niketown/Nike retail stores, the SNKRS app for limited drops, and nike.com direct-to-consumer. In 2019 Nike stopped selling directly through Amazon to focus on direct customer relationships.
